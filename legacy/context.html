Context for Project

Understanding the purpose, background, and vision behind the LionKing project — and why it matters.

Background

Why This Project Exists

The LionKing project was created to raise awareness about the African lion — one of the most iconic yet increasingly endangered species on the planet. Through engaging web design and compelling facts, this project aims to educate visitors and inspire conservation action.

Lion populations have declined by over 40% in the past two decades due to habitat loss, human-wildlife conflict, and poaching. This project serves as a digital tribute to these magnificent creatures and a call to action for their protection.

Mission

Project Goals

💡

Inspire

🤝
